EF Game

If A and B are n-equivalent for every n, no first-order sentence is true in one and false in the other. A winning strategy for Spoiler in EFn(A, B) converts into a first-order sentence, with quantifiers nested at most n deep, that is true in exactly one of them. These games give a necessary and sufficient condition for elementary equivalence. Read more
